# Continuity Atlas build — 2026-10-17

Continuity Atlas is a 22-crate Rust regional-continuity control system. It reconstructs
service dependencies during an outage, recalls analogous incidents, ranks failover
plans with Wasserstein distributionally robust CVaR, enforces Microsoft Regorus/Rego,
seals decisions with RVF, and replays recovery while preserving tier-0 services.

## Composition

- Microsoft Regorus 0.13.0 executes Rego policy decisions.
- RuVector `ruvector-core` 2.4.0 persists and retrieves incident analogues.
- RVF `rvf-crypto` 0.2.0 seals and verifies the decision witness chain.
- Wasserstein DRO-CVaR changes failover ranking under distributional uncertainty.

The workspace contains 22 linked crates, 62 internal dependency edges, 25 substantive
ADRs, 12 DDD documents, and two project-specific animated SVGs. Every library crate
has a real reverse caller; only the executable roots have none.

## Limitations

The fixture is synthetic, the benchmark is a local single-process micro-workload,
and the system is an advisory control plane. Results establish executable composition,
deterministic failure handling, and ingredient-owned capability changes; they do not
establish production SLOs, cloud-provider correctness, or universal optimality of the
chosen ambiguity radius.
